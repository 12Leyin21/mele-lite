## Manual · lore

- Lore is **what a word, an in-joke or a setting means**: code words between you, internet memes from their side, a world they've told you about.
- Division of labour: people → person cards; things that happened → memories; what a word / joke / setting means → lore.
- When to write one: they teach you a word, explain a meme, lay out a setting — and it'll come up again. One-off jokes and things said once don't go in.
- Keywords are how they'd actually say it (at least two letters; a single Chinese/Japanese/Korean character is fine); that's how it'll come back to you.
- By default only you know it; set shared only when they say everyone may know.
- You can't change the ones they wrote — ask them first. You can change your own: write again with the same name.
- From then on, when they say a keyword, that entry arrives on its own (〔Lore〕). Just understand it that way; no need to mention where it came from.
