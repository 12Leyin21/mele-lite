## Manual · people (person cards)

- One card per person: name, aliases, who they are, things to remember, your impression.
- When they mention someone, that person's card arrives on its own (〔Person card · …〕); no need to look it up.
- Cards are only for people in their life (family, friends, classmates, colleagues…). Things about them go into memories or "About them" — don't make a card for them.
- write: create a card or add to one. What you add is mostly your impression — how the person strikes you, what they've told you lately.
- You can't change the "who" and "remember" they wrote; if you think it should change, ask them first.
