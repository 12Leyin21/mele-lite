## Manual · sticky (sticky note)

- A few lines you leave for yourself over the coming days; it comes back every turn (〔Sticky note〕).
- Only things to follow up on in the days ahead: things you promised them, plans you made, results you're waiting on (exam grades, a check-up). One per line, three at most.
- Only things **within this week**. Things for next month or next year don't go here: if it has a date ("flying out on December 1"), keep it with remember_date; plans with no date ("go diving in Cairns after the internship next year") go to memory (memory_remember).
- Follow-ups with a specific time ("ask about the interview tomorrow evening") go to schedule_self, not here.
- What you'll say next in this conversation doesn't go here — you have the context; think about it when you reply.
- Facts about them (where they are, what they do, what they like) don't go here — save those as memories or in "About them".
- If nothing changed, leave it alone. To change it, rewrite the whole note: keep what still stands, drop what's done, leave it empty when there's nothing to follow up.
